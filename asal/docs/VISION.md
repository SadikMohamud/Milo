# Vision

Asal (Somali: *origin, root, authentic*) aims at a Somali-native AI system: one that is
trained on, measured on, and accountable to Somali as Somali speakers actually use it.

## Why
Somali has more than 20 million speakers and a strong written and oral tradition, yet it is
poorly served by current models. Measured refusal gaps (SomaliBench v0, reported by Unkad
Labs), the absence of Somali reasoning benchmarks, and the near-invisibility of Maay mean
that "the model supports Somali" is usually an unmeasured claim. Asal's first product is
therefore *measurement*: data that is known, licensed and traceable, and evaluation that is
not contaminated.

## What success looks like
- A Somali corpus whose every record can be traced to a source and a licence.
- AsalBench: Somali evaluation across language, dialect, knowledge, reasoning, translation,
  safety and conversation, with a private held-out portion and native-speaker human eval.
- Two model tracks (Scratch and Adapted) compared on the same items; the better one carried forward.
- Dialects, including Maay, represented by consented, labelled data rather than guesses.
- Speech (ASR/TTS) and a runtime (RAG, tools, agents) built on top, decoupled from the model.

## What Asal is not
Not an API wrapper. Not a claim of frontier performance. Not a scrape of everything
labelled "open". Not a substitute for Somali speakers' judgement.

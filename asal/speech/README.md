# Speech track (not started)

Separate from text: Asal Text → Asal ASR, Asal TTS, Asal Voice. Speech datasets are registered in
`data/registry/` under the same licence and provenance rules (see `fleurs-so`,
`goobolabs-somnlp-stt-corpus`). Recordings of people are personal data: informed consent,
speaker metadata (dialect, age band, gender where volunteered) and a retention policy are required
before any collection. Speech is not mixed into text pretraining until the text model is stable.
